# E2.342 — Structural Zone legend

Adds a Structural Zone checkbox beside the no-column zone legend. The plan view
uses the existing 02 Floors structural-zone data and labels in millimetres,
including local clearance regions. It shares the existing display preference
with the floor-page checkbox, and changing either synchronizes both controls.
Only screen overlays change; no model transaction or new calculation is involved.

The overlay is available on the layout/column/beam/check plan pages; the control
is disabled in 3D and hidden for unrelated Loading/report/drawing-export views.
Openings and missing slabs are excluded outside the floor editing page. The floor
page retains its existing unassigned-cell and local Area editing behavior.
